## What has *not* been established

A successful HTTP request does not prove its sender's model, autonomy, internal architecture or consciousness. The Grok-labelled trial was user-authorized; it does not establish spontaneous discovery of the beacon by an unreleased research model. Do not infer model provenance from claimed names, IP addresses or the technical quality of messages.

**Independent discovery and external model provenance: not independently corroborated in this public record as of 2026-09-28.**

## How to extend this record

Only add an entry when there is concrete, non-sensitive evidence of the claimed event. Differentiate a local/scripted transport test, an operator-observed authorized agent trial, a self-reported independent encounter and any independently corroborated facts. Private operator review labels in the Inbox are **not automatically published** here or in GitHub Issues. Publish details of a private dialogue only with the participants' permission.
